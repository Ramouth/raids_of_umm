# Known failures before the rework (2026-10-07)

At e0d1a8d and 6c69895 the verifier reports NOT VERIFIED. Every failure is in the old northern
demo (`old_passage`) and comes from two causes. Neither comes from the iteration 1 work: the
same failures appear at the checkpoint commit.

## Cause 1: the generated story file is out of date with its source

`data/maps/old_passage.triggers.json` is generated by `scripts/north_story.py`. Regenerating it
into a scratch directory and comparing shows exactly one difference, the `intro` trigger:

- The source (north_story.py:66-70) opens with two Steward lines: "Lord Aldren's guest is
  waiting on the west road. Five wolf packs have closed the vale." and "Clear the road and bring
  her safely to Varenhold."
- The committed JSON has only the quest and the "Families" lore entry. The Steward never speaks.

So either the JSON was edited by hand, or the source changed after the last generation.
**Fails:** `tests/test_level_one.cpp:83` (`spoke(lines, "Steward")`).

## Cause 2: the vale now has five wolf packs; the tests and the bot clear three

`vale_cleared` (north_story.py:93) requires Hill, Den, Hermit's, **Lowland and Thornwood**
Wolves, and the map has all five. The tests and `demo_bot` still clear only the first three
(test_level_one.cpp:91-102, demo_bot.cpp:175). The vale is never cleared, so:

- no +40 XP and no "west road is clear" line;
- Ushari never arrives that night, and the `ushari_at_night` choice never appears;
- everything that needs Ushari fails after that: her readings of the gate runes, the survey,
  both endings.

**Fails:**
- test_level_one.cpp:110, :155, :159, :205-207, :214 and :402-403 (`readings == 3`, Ushari
  speaking).
- **The crash:** test_level_one.cpp:264 reads `s.specials()[0].xp` while the list is empty.
  That is the segmentation fault (exit 139) in raids_tests and native adventure_regression.
- demo_smoke's 3 failures: "Clearing the vale reaches level 2", "Level 2 asks for a path" and
  "The path screen opens from the level-up pop-up". All of them depend on the vale's XP.
- All four demo_bot routes end INCOMPLETE at the survey ("Read the inscriptions … with Ushari
  first"), because Ushari never joined.
- The blind player's first run read this broken story (no Ushari arrival, then about 90 idle
  days), so some of its "lost" findings come from these bugs, not from the design.

## What we do about it

Nothing. The old demo retires when chapter 1 replaces it (decided 2026-10-07), and its tests
retire with it. Two lessons carry into chapter 1:

1. **A generated file must not drift from its source.** verify.sh should regenerate the story
   and maps into a scratch directory and fail if they differ from the committed files.
2. **The bot and the tests must take their objectives from the story data** (for example, the
   pack names listed in a `cleared` trigger), not from hand-written lists that go stale.
