# Map density — HoMM3 as the default

How much "stuff" a map should hold, derived from Heroes of Might and Magic III and
converted to our hex maps. Use these numbers as the starting point for every stage;
deviate on purpose, not by accident.

## 1. HoMM3 reference numbers

**Movement.** A hero has roughly 1 500 movement points a day (1 360–2 000 depending on
the slowest creature). Costs per step: grass/dirt 100, rough 125, sand/snow 150,
swamp 175; roads 50–75; a diagonal step costs ×√2. So a hero walks
**~15 open tiles a day**, ~20–30 on roads.

**Map sizes.** S 36×36 (1 296 tiles), M 72×72, L 108×108, XL 144×144.
Roughly **40–50 % of tiles are obstacles** (trees, rocks, mountains, water), so an S map
has ~700 walkable tiles.

**Object census.** These counts are *estimates* from typical random-map output and
hand-made S maps for two players, not an exact count. Per player zone of about 160
walkable tiles:

| Kind | Per zone | Notes |
|------|---------:|-------|
| Monster stacks (guards) | 8–12 | Each has a zone of control: its 8 neighbours |
| Resource piles, campfires, chests | 10–15 | Chest: 1 000 gold **or** 500 XP (up to 2 000/1 500) |
| Mines | 4–6 | Wood and ore near the town; rare resources further out |
| Weekly / one-time sites | 4–6 | Windmill, water wheel, stables, learning stone, redwood observatory, obelisk |
| Creature dwellings | 1–2 | Usually guarded |
| Artifacts | 2–4 | Usually guarded |
| Town | 1 | |
| **Total interactables** | **~35** | **≈ 1 per 4.5 walkable tiles** |

**Activity per day.** A 15-tile day passes within reach of 6–9 objects, and a player
actually *takes* 2–4 of them: one fight, one or two pickups, sometimes a site.

## 2. Converting to hexes

- **Our day** is 10–11 movement (DEFAULT_MOVES 10 plus Ushari's +1) at grass cost 1.0, so
  **~10 hexes a day**. That is 2/3 of the HoMM3 day.
- **Neighbours.** A hex has 6 neighbours instead of 8, so one hex covers roughly the same
  "reach" as one HoMM3 tile. A walked hex sweeps about 3 hexes of reach (itself plus its sides).
- To keep HoMM3's **2–4 objects taken per day** with a shorter day, spacing must shrink
  by about the same 2/3. Target: **1 interactable per 5–6 walkable hexes**, not 4.5, because
  our guards block (zone of control) and our days are shorter. A denser map stalls.
- **Guard share.** In HoMM3 about 30 % of interactables are monsters, and in practice every
  valuable (artifact, dwelling, chest, mine) has a stack next to it.

## 4. Checking a map

1. `python3 scripts/gen_north_map.py`: prints walkable hexes and objects per walkable hex.
2. `godot/native/build/battle_sim data '<army>' '<guards>' 20`: each guard against the army
   you expect when the player first meets it (see ROADMAP demo notes).
3. `godot/native/build/demo_bot . 12`: a cautious greedy player. It walks home every week
   to recruit, so a human should be faster. It is a floor, not a forecast.
